Why This Project

Attackers no longer rely only on suspicious emails. They hide links in QR codes, copy trusted brands, and reuse passwords leaked from older breaches. This project puts one check for each of those problems in one place.

The problems it targets

Phishing links

Fake login pages are built to look like banks, shops and social networks. A link can look harmless until you read it closely.

Quishing

A QR code hides its destination. People scan posters, flyers and emails without seeing where they lead.

Weak or leaked passwords

Many accounts are taken over with passwords that are short, predictable, or already exposed in a breach.

What the project aims to do

  • Give anyone a fast answer about a link, with a confidence score.
  • Let people check a QR code before they open what is inside it.
  • Explain why a password is weak and how to improve it.
  • Keep it simple enough that no technical knowledge is needed.

Who it is for

Students, small business owners, and family members who are not security experts but use links, QR codes and passwords every day.
